# Multi-city

Cities are managed data (`settings.cities: City[]`), not hard-coded lists. Rules live in `packages/shared/src/cities.ts` and are mirrored with identical messages in `backend/.../settings/CityService.java`.

## Model
`City { name, state, status, lat, lng, radiusKm, areas[], disabledCategoryIds[] }` (types.ts).
- `name` is the key: addresses, customers, vendors and bookings store the city by name, so cities can't be renamed. Lookups are case-insensitive.
- `status`: `live` (customers book), `coming_soon` (vendors can sign up and be verified; customers can't book), `paused` (no new bookings or sign-ups; existing bookings continue).
- `radiusKm`: "Use current location" only matches a live city whose centre is within this radius.
- `areas`: localities vendors pick as service areas.
- `disabledCategoryIds`: services not offered in that city (everything else enabled platform-wide is).
- Delete only works while nothing references the city; otherwise pause it.

## Rules
- Booking (`createBooking` / `checkout`, mock and backend): the address city must be live and offer the category (`bookingBlockedReason`).
- Vendor sign-up: live or coming-soon city; the stored name is canonicalised.
- Matching (`suggestVendors`, `VendorMatcher` SQL): only approved vendors whose city equals the booking's city.

## Screens
- Admin `/cities` (permission `settings`): cards with status, supply (approved/pending vendors), customers, bookings (+ needing a professional), revenue, services not offered; `CitySheet` to add/edit/delete. Vendor picker copy says "All in {city}".
- Customer: picker lists live cities + "Coming soon: …"; `useCatalog().services` hides services the city doesn't offer (`allServices`/`getService` still find them); `CityNotice` banner on home/service pages; cart blocks checkout with the reason; a picked city that was deleted falls back to the first live one.
- Vendor: register shows live + coming-soon cities (with a "launching soon" note), areas from the city; profile suggests the city's other localities.

## Backend
- `V2__multi_city.sql`: status/state/radius columns, `platform_city_areas`, `platform_city_disabled_categories`, index on `lower(vendors.city)`.
- Dev data: `db/dev/V1001__dev_city_details.sql` (generated by `npm run backend:seed`, which also rewrites V1000 — restore V1000 from git unless you intend to reseed; see the generator comment). The dev profile sets Flyway `out-of-order: true` so existing dev databases apply V2 after V1000.
- API: `POST /api/v1/admin/cities`, `PUT /api/v1/admin/cities/{name}`, `DELETE /api/v1/admin/cities/{name}`; cities come to clients in `/api/v1/sync` → `settings.cities`. Snapshot/mock DB version 8.
- Tests: `CitiesIT` (5 tests).

## City pricing (2026-10-10)
Rules in `cities.ts` ("City pricing" section), mirrored by `backend/.../pricing/CityPricing.java` with the same test cases on both sides.
- `City.priceLevel` (whole %, 50–300, default 100): `cityPrice(base, level)` = base × level%, rounded to the nearest amount ending in 9 (tie goes up, minimum ₹9). Level 100 and free (₹0) prices are returned untouched. Whole-number arithmetic so TS and Java agree.
- `ProblemType.cityPrices[cityName]`: an exact price for one problem in one city, charged as typed (no rounding). `problemPriceIn(problem, city)` = exact price ?? level price. Set/cleared with `admin.setCityPrice(problemTypeId, city, price | null)` → `PUT /api/v1/admin/problem-types/{id}/city-prices/{city}` body `{ price }` (permission `services`). Deleting a city drops its exact prices.
- Inspection fee: `inspectionFeeIn(category, settings, city)` = category/default fee at the city's level (no exact override).
- `City.commissionRate` (optional, 0–0.6): `commissionRateIn(category, settings, city)` = city rate ?? category rate ?? default.
- Booking creation (mock `createBooking`, backend `CustomerBookingService`) prices items, the inspection fee and commission from the **visit address's city**.
- Existing bookings keep their price: items and the commission rate are stored on the booking; repricing after an inspection quote (`repriced` / `BookingCore.reprice`) reuses the booking's stored commission rate instead of the category's current one.
- Customer app: `buildServices(db, city)` / `useCatalog()` show the selected city's prices; the service page says "Prices shown are for {city}". Cart items remember the city they were added in (`CartItem.city`); if the address city gives a different item total the cart shows "Prices updated for {city}" with old and new totals, and OK re-stamps the items (`cart.acceptPrices`).
- Admin: price level + city commission in `CitySheet` and on the Cities cards. Services list and detail take the city filter (`<Page cityFilter>` + `useCityScope`): prices, inspection fee, commission and the example breakdown are that city's; `ProblemSheet` gets a `city` prop and an "Exact price in {city}" field.
- Backend: `V3__city_pricing.sql` (`platform_cities.price_level`, `commission_rate`, table `problem_type_city_prices`), dev data `V1002__dev_city_pricing.sql`. Snapshot/mock DB version 9.
- Seed: Pune 110, Mumbai 125 (+22% commission), Bengaluru 120, Hyderabad 110, Delhi 125; el-fan is exactly ₹349 in Mumbai. Seeded bookings keep base prices.
- Tests: `npm test` (`packages/shared/test/city-pricing.test.ts`, 13 tests over the rules and the mock), `CityPricingTest` (6) and `CityPricingIT` (6) in the backend.
